The table groups by cause from the code, not by subject. Two causes fall out — parser and hint — They are two separate problems, with two separate fixes.

Problem 1 — the shared parser reads dates month first

The sched-prod parser reads every stored date as MM/DD. A day-first tenant's 07/04 (7 April) comes back as 4 July: day and month trade places. This is the known bug, and it is the bulk of the ticket — 379 reports, the whole parser group. It reaches the desk through every channel and every tenant, because every tenant on file writes the day first. Fix the parser and these stop.

This is the only problem the queued fix addresses. Problem 2 survives it.

Five of these parser reports (desk-notes.json → oneDayMove, footnoted by the table above) describe a date that is one day off, not swapped — "only June" (a DST boundary), "Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday, same meeting" (a time zone), "the invite arrived before I made the booking" (a UTC offset). The desk files them under parser by tenant. Watch them as a possible day-boundary bug, but they are not a cause of their own.

Problem 2 — booking-v3 prints the wrong date hint

Under its date field, the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY. A day-first tenant's staff follow the hint and type the month first; the form then stores the date read their way, day first — so it is swapped before it ever reaches the parser. Same wrong day on the invite, different root cause. These come only through the form, and only from the three tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour). The parser fix does nothing here: the fix is the form's hint. causeOf already separates them — channel === 'form' on a MM-hint form is tagged hint, not parser.
